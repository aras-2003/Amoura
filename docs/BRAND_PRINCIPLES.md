# Brand Principles

## Desired character

Amoura should feel:
- premium,
- warm,
- discreet,
- contemporary,
- emotionally intelligent,
- mature without feeling old-fashioned.

## Avoid

Do not use:
- vulgar or pornographic visual language,
- infantilizing femininity,
- stereotypical pink/purple adult-store aesthetics,
- neon-heavy presentation,
- clichés about menopause, age or relationships,
- provocative imagery as a shortcut to relevance.

## Visual direction

Prefer:
- editorial composition,
- refined typography,
- warm materials and tones,
- premium photography,
- restrained sensuality,
- elegant spacing,
- subtle depth and motion.

The brand should feel safe enough for trust and distinctive enough to avoid clinical blandness.
