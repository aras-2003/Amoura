# UX Principles

## Core UX goals

Users should be able to:
- understand what Amoura offers quickly,
- browse discreetly,
- find products without catalogue overload,
- understand why a product is relevant,
- access education without feeling judged,
- complete key flows with low cognitive effort.

## Audience implications

Design for strong readability and confidence:
- generous font sizes,
- clear contrast,
- large tap targets,
- predictable navigation,
- concise forms,
- understandable errors,
- clear privacy and delivery information.

## Commerce

Favor curated decision support over endless filtering.

Product pages should explain:
- purpose,
- fit,
- material or usage information where appropriate,
- relevant precautions,
- privacy/discretion aspects,
- delivery expectations.

## Prototype honesty

Mock integrations must be visibly distinguishable from real production capabilities.
