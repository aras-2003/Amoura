# Product

## Current stage

Amoura is a concept-stage sexual-wellness brand and digital commerce experience.

It is not currently represented by this repository as an operating company or established commercial business.

## Core proposition

Build a trusted, curated sexual-wellness experience that combines commerce with education, guidance and community rather than behaving like a conventional adult-products catalogue.

## Primary audience

Mature women, including women 45+, who value discretion, trust, quality, understandable guidance and a non-judgmental experience.

## Product pillars

The experience may combine:
- curated commerce,
- education and editorial content,
- guidance and discovery,
- community,
- selected events or offline experiences.

## Product principles

- trust before conversion pressure;
- curation before catalogue scale;
- discretion by default;
- understandable guidance before feature density;
- no unsupported health claims;
- prototype behavior must be labeled honestly;
- accessibility is part of product quality.

## Stage discipline

Do not over-engineer unvalidated features.

Separate hypotheses from validated requirements and production-ready capabilities.
